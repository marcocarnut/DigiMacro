# Micronucleus 2.6

`t85_default.hex` is the ATtiny85 build of the Micronucleus bootloader,
release v2.6, unmodified: `firmware/releases/t85_default.hex` at tag `v2.6` of
<https://github.com/micronucleus/micronucleus> (sha256 `bb104682…94573`).
Micronucleus is GPLv2, as DigiMacro is; its source is at that tag.

It is the bootloader a Digispark comes with, and the uninstaller
(`app/uninstall.c`) carries it home: it occupies 0x1A00 to 0x1FE9.
